LinkedData.es

In this web, you will find the major initiatives, applications, websites and collaborations developed by the OEG. You will also find tools, awards and other initiatives no longer maintained.

datos.ign.es

Geographical domain

datos.ign.es Initiative of the National Geographic Institute (IGN) for the generation of semantic information of its resources in which the OEG has participated.

datos.bne.es

Culture domain

datos.bne.es Initiative from the project "Linked data at the BNE", supported by the Spanish National Library (BNE) in cooperation with the OEG. The BNE publishes bibliographic and authority data in RDF following Linked Data principles and under the CC0 open license.

esDBpedia

Host of esDBpedia

esDBpedia Spanish chapter of DBpedia hosted by OEG. It contains semantic information extracted from Spanish Wikipedia. Its SPARQL endpoint exposes around 170 million relevant triples.

Open Data Zaragoza

Smart Cities and Open Data

Open Data Zaragoza Zaragoza City Hall initiative to promote reuse of the information published on its website by citizens, companies and other institutions. OEG supports development and vocabulary definitions.

vocab.linkeddata.es

Ontology services

vocab.linkeddata.es Catalogue of vocabularies that the Ontology Engineering Group (OEG) is developing and publishing on the Web.

vocab.linkeddata.es/datosabiertos

Smart Cities and Open Data

vocab.linkeddata.es/datosabiertos Catalogue of vocabularies created at OpenCityData for homogeneous publication of open data from cities in Spain.

SmartCity Catalogues

Smart Cities and Open Data

SmartCity Catalogues Catalogue of ontologies and datasets about smart cities, energy and related fields, developed within the Ready4SmartCities European Project.

Terminesp Linked Data

Linguistic

Terminesp Linked Data Initiative to transform lexical information from the Terminesp terminological database into RDF following Linked Data best practices.

Apertium RDF

Linguistic

Apertium RDF RDF version of the Apertium bilingual dictionaries, transformed into RDF and published on the Web following Linked Data principles.

RDF License dataset

Ontology services

RDF License dataset RDF representation of different licenses for data, software and general works.
