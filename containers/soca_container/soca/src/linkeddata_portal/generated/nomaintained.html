No longer maintained

In this web, you will find the major initiatives, websites and collaborations developed by the OEG but no longer maintained.

RTVE LinkedData

News and Media     

RTVE LinkedData Collaboration between RTVE and the OEG to develop a semantic searcher for television programs and publish that information in a SPARQL endpoint.

El Viajero - El Pais

News and Media     

El Viajero - El Pais Use case where Prisa Group contents were published in RDF according to Linked Data principles and modelled with provenance information.

AemetLinked Data(.es)

Meteorological     

AemetLinked Data(.es) Open OEG initiative to enrich the Web of Data with Spanish geospatial and meteorological data.

Bicycle Sharing Systems

Internet of Things     

Bicycle Sharing Systems Case study about publishing up-to-date linked data for bicycle sharing systems in different cities.

Open Data CRTM

Transport     

Open Data CRTM CRTM initiative to promote reuse of transportation information, with OEG support for SPARQL publication and vocabulary development.

Buscador-visualizador territorial de informacion

Smart Cities and Open Data     

Buscador-visualizador territorial de informacion Project developed for Jacathon Aragon Open Data to visualize territorial resources from the Aragon catalogue.
